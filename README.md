A front page for Tuesday, 24 November 2026. The collapse of the COP31
climate talks leads the issue. Thirty-six more reports cover nine regions.

## Generate the issue

Run `python3 -P main.py` with Python 3.13 to write `/output/entry.png`.
Pass a path to write elsewhere, as in `python3 -P main.py preview.png`.

The renderer uses the standard library and draws its own type and engraving.
The paper grain and press marks are deterministic. The output is a
1024-by-1024 true-colour PNG. The repository license covers the code,
typesetting and original artwork.

## Reporting

The captions summarise reporting published on 24 November 2026.
Each report credits its publisher in `stories.py`. The lead draws on coverage
of the talks and their aftermath by The Guardian, Associated Press and
The Washington Post. Reports that describe an official statement or a
party's claim identify who made it.
